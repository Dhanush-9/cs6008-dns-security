================================================================
WHY THE ATTACK SUCCEEDS
================================================================

1. Plain DNS has no authentication:
   - DNS responses carry no cryptographic signature
   - A resolver accepts the first response matching TXID + src-port + qname
   - The forged packet has identical TXID and correct src IP/port (spoofed)

2. On-path advantage:
   - Attacker sits between auth servers and resolver (via core-router divert)
   - Sees genuine packets BEFORE resolver
   - Can inject forged response with sub-millisecond advantage
   - Race condition is always won because genuine packet is also relayed
     but arrives as a duplicate (resolver ignores second response)

3. Bailiwick constraint satisfied:
   - Attacker only forges A records for example.com from domain-ns (172.16.3.30)
   - NS referrals and www.example.com pass through unchanged
   - This prevents breaking the iterative resolution chain
